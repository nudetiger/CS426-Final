\section{Screen gallery}
\label{sec:screens}

All captures come from the signed release APK running on a Pixel emulator at API~36 with the gateway
live against DeepSeek. Nothing is a mock-up: the Distributed Systems branch visible in several shots
was generated by the assistant from the single request quoted in \S\ref{sec:ai}. \textbf{Review
changes} is the one to look at twice --- six valid operations ticked, one the model invented left
unticked with its reason, and nothing yet written to the database.
